Why so many multi-joints? Why show the same multi-joint twice in the same style? And here we have the fundamental feature of the new macro.
1119348150946The main new advantage of this new macro is it allows you to differentiate the panels according to they are internal or external.
This is the reason we have increased the number of multi-joints.

This may at first seem an inconvenience, but in reality it has several advantages.

Firstly, you can differentiate the left vertical section from the right (which previously was not possible) and this is reason for the duplicated multi-joints on the vertical sections.

The possibility to easily switch the tracks of the sliding system from internal to external (not possible with a single multi-joint).

Correct management of thermal performance is now possible, as you can distinguish the internal sliding panel from the external.

Let&apos;s consider the above points in more detail.

The so-called‘Parametric’function can be specified for each type of fitting by pressing the button bearing the same name. Thanks to this function, application of a fitting can be conditioned on the basis of two different systems: therange system  and the distance system .
1985259152631

Range s ystem:the system can be set so that a fitting is inserted only when a certain
dimension of the opening ( Perimeter , Width , Height, 2H+L (3 sides),a
Side, or a formula ) lies between two presettable limits. Up to 2 conditions are available.

Distance system:the program can be made to automatically calculate the multiplier number of fittings on the basis of a maximum distance to be considered on a check
